## 4. Booking (renters)

4.1 You choose the pick-up and return days and send a booking request. The full price is shown
before you send it.

4.2 When you send the request, the amount is **reserved (held)** on your card. It is not charged.

4.3 The owner has up to 6 days to answer, and less when the pick-up day is sooner. If the owner
says yes, the booking is confirmed and your card is charged. If the owner says no or doesn't
answer in time, the hold is released and you pay nothing.

4.4 The person who books is the renter and is responsible for the item during the rental, also
when someone else uses it.

## 5. Pick-up and return

5.1 The renter and the owner agree on the pick-up and return time and place in Messages. The
exact address is shown to the renter after the owner accepts.

5.2 At pick-up, check the item together and take photos. The owner hands over the item as
described, with everything listed under "What you get".

5.3 The renter uses the item with care, for its normal purpose and following the owner's
instructions, doesn't lend it on, and returns it on time, clean and in the same condition.

5.4 **Late return:** tell the owner as soon as you know you'll be late. The owner can ask for the
day price for each late day. **[Decision]** Hygglo charges this automatically from the renter's
card; DragonRent can't, so late days are agreed between renter and owner, with our help if needed.

## 6. Prices, fees and payment

6.1 The price is the owner's price for the booked days. Longer rentals can use the owner's lower
3-day or 7-day price; the total shown at checkout is what the renter pays.

6.2 **Renters pay no service fee.** **[Decision]** Hygglo adds a booking fee for renters and
Airbnb a guest service fee; if DragonRent adds one, it is shown before the request is sent.

6.3 Owners pay DragonRent a commission of [15]% of the rental price (at least €[1]), taken before
payout. **[Decision: fee level.]**

6.4 Payments are handled by Stripe. DragonRent never sees full card numbers.

6.5 The owner's payout is sent through Stripe two days after the return day. Stripe then needs a
few working days to reach the owner's bank.

6.6 All payments for a rental must go through DragonRent. Asking for or making payments outside
DragonRent is not allowed and can lead to account closure.

## 7. Cancellations and refunds

7.1 The full rules are in the [Cancellation policy](/p/cancellation-policy).

7.2 Before the owner accepts, the renter pays nothing (section 4.3).

7.3 After the owner accepts, a booking can be cancelled by contacting us. When DragonRent cancels
a booking, the renter gets a full refund to the card used.

7.4 **[Decision]** Hygglo and Airbnb let each owner choose a cancellation policy (for example free
cancellation until a set time before pick-up) and let users cancel in the app. DragonRent
currently can't do this without a change to its booking system; until then the rule in 7.3
applies.

7.5 **Right of withdrawal:** by sending a booking request, the renter asks for the rental to start
on the chosen day and accepts that the 14-day right of withdrawal for distance contracts doesn't
apply to a rental for a fixed date. [Lawyer: confirm wording; Hygglo uses a similar clause.]

## 8. Damage, loss and theft

8.1 The full rules are in the [Damage policy](/p/damage-policy).

8.2 The renter is responsible for loss of the item, theft, and damage caused on purpose or by
carelessness, or by use against the owner's instructions. Normal wear is not damage.

8.3 Report damage, loss or theft to the owner in Messages straight away, and to us within [7]
days of the return day.

8.4 **[Decision]** Hygglo includes damage insurance in many categories, and Airbnb offers its own
damage protection for hosts. DragonRent offers **no insurance or deposit** at launch. Decide who
bears accidental damage that isn't anyone's fault (usually the owner or the owner's own insurance),
and recommend that owners check their home insurance.

8.5 If an item isn't returned and the renter can't be reached, we may pass the renter's details to
the owner and the police. [Decision: Hygglo can charge a deposit up to the item's value in this
