## 4. Listing an item (owners)

- Only list items you own or have the right to rent out, that are safe and legal to rent, and that
  match your photos and description.
- You set the price for 1 day, and you can set lower prices for 3 and 7 days.
- Before your listing is published, you must connect a Stripe account so we can pay you. Stripe
  checks your identity.
- You choose which days the item is available, and you answer each request with yes or no.

## 5. Booking an item (renters)

1. You choose the pick-up and return days and send a request. The full price is shown before you
   send it.
2. When you send the request, the amount is **held** on your card. It is not charged yet.
3. The owner has up to 6 days to answer (less if the pick-up day is sooner).
4. If the owner says yes, your card is **charged** and the booking is confirmed.
5. If the owner says no or doesn't answer in time, the hold is released and you pay nothing.

You and the owner agree on the pick-up time and place in Messages. The exact address is shown
after the owner accepts.
