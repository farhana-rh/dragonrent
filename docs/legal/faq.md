# Frequently asked questions

Not legal text, but check the answers match the reviewed terms before publishing.

## Renting

**When is my card charged?**
When you send a request, the amount is held on your card. It's charged only if the owner says
yes. If they say no or don't answer in time, the hold is released and you pay nothing.

**How long does the owner have to answer?**
Up to 6 days, less if your pick-up day is sooner.

**Why can't I see the exact address?**
For the owner's privacy, the item page shows only the area. You see the address after the owner
accepts, and you agree on the pick-up time in Messages.

**Why is a longer rental cheaper per day?**
Owners can set lower prices for 3 and 7 days. The price for your dates is worked out
automatically and shown before you send the request.

**Can I cancel?**
Contact us. See the [Cancellation policy](/p/cancellation-policy).

## Lending

**What do I need to start lending?**
An account, photos of your item, a day price, where it can be picked up and the days it's free.
Before your item is published, you connect a Stripe account so we can pay you.

**When do I get paid?**
Two days after the return day, the money is sent to your Stripe account. Stripe then needs a few
working days to move it to your bank.

**How much does DragonRent take?**
[Open decision: commission. Currently 15% of the rental price, at least €1.]

**What if my item gets damaged?**
See the [Damage policy](/p/damage-policy).

## Account

**Can I both rent and lend?**
Yes, one account does both.

**How do I close my account?**
In Account settings → Manage account.
