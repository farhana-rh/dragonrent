# Cancellation policy

> **Draft, needs legal review.** Not yet valid. Last updated [date].
>
> **Open decision (docs/SCOPE.md):** the cancellation rule. This draft describes what the booking
> system does today: only DragonRent can cancel an accepted booking, always with a full refund.
> If you want rules such as "free until 2 days before pick-up", they must be written here and
> applied by hand by the person who cancels, or the booking process must be changed.

## Before the owner says yes

- You haven't been charged, only a hold placed on your card.
- If the owner says no, or doesn't answer in time (up to 6 days, less if the pick-up day is
  sooner), the request ends and the hold is released. You pay nothing.
- To withdraw a request before the owner answers, contact us at [email]. [Check: the default
  booking process has no "withdraw" for renters; support would ask the owner to decline.]

## After the owner says yes

- You can't cancel in the app. Contact us at [email] as early as you can, with the booking
  details.
- **If you (the renter) need to cancel:** [rule to decide, e.g. full refund if cancelled at
  least 2 days before pick-up; after that, at the owner's discretion].
- **If the owner needs to cancel:** you always get a full refund.
- When we cancel a booking, the renter gets a **full refund** to the card used. Stripe usually
  returns the money within 5–10 working days.
