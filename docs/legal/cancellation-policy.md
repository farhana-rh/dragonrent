## In short

- You can cancel for free **within 24 hours of sending your booking request**.
- **After 24 hours, the booking can't be cancelled** and the rental price isn't refunded, in full
  or in part.
- If the owner cancels, or doesn't hand over the item, you always get all your money back.

## How to cancel

You can't cancel in the app. Email us at [email] within 24 hours of sending your request, with
the item's name and the booking dates. The time your email reaches us counts. [Check: times are
Finnish time.]

## Within 24 hours

- **If the owner hasn't answered yet:** we withdraw your request. The hold on your card is
  released and you pay nothing.
- **If the owner has already accepted:** we cancel the booking and refund the full amount to the
  card you used. Stripe usually returns the money within 5–10 working days.

## After 24 hours

- We don't cancel the booking, and the rental price isn't refunded. The owner is paid as usual.
- This is different from a request that ends without a booking: if the owner says no, or doesn't
  answer in time (up to 6 days, less if the pick-up day is sooner), the hold on your card is
  released and you pay nothing.

## If the owner cancels

If the owner can't rent the item after accepting, contact us. We cancel the booking and you get a
**full refund**, at any time.
