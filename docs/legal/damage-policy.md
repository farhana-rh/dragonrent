## Before the rental

- **Owners:** describe the item honestly, including any wear or faults, and add photos.
- **Renters and owners:** at pick-up, check the item together and take photos. Send them to each
  other in Messages so both have a record with a date.

## If something breaks, goes missing or comes back late

1. Tell the other person in Messages as soon as possible.
2. Agree on a fair solution between yourselves, for example repair costs or a reduced price.
3. If you can't agree, contact us at [email] within [7 days] of the return day, with photos and
   the messages.

## What DragonRent does

- We can look at the booking, the messages and the photos, and suggest a solution.
- [Lawyer: whether DragonRent can withhold or adjust the owner's payout or charge the renter;

- The renter is responsible for damage caused by careless use and for loss of the item, up to
  [the item's current value]. Normal wear is not damage. [Lawyer: confirm.]
