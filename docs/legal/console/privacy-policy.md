## 1. Who is responsible

[Company name], business ID [Y-tunnus], [address], is the controller of the personal data in DragonRent. Contact for privacy questions: [email].

## 2. What we collect

- **Name, email, phone (optional) and password:** from you at sign-up, for your account and emails about bookings.
- **Profile picture and text:** from you, shown on your profile and listings.
- **Listings (photos, descriptions, prices, availability, address):** from owners, to show and book items.
- **Booking requests, dates, prices and reviews:** from you and the other user, to run bookings.
- **Messages between users:** from you, so renters and owners can agree on details.
- **Card payments:** through Stripe. We don't see or store full card numbers.
- **Owners' identity and bank details:** through Stripe, for payouts and the checks Stripe must do by law.
- **Technical data (browser, pages, errors):** from your device, to keep the service working and secure.
- **Visit statistics:** from your device, **only if you allow analytics**, to improve the service.

## 3. Legal bases

- Contract: running your account and bookings.
- Legal obligation: bookkeeping and payment-service rules.
- Legitimate interest: security, fraud prevention, error monitoring, customer support.
- Consent: analytics cookies. You can withdraw it at any time under "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

## 4. Who we share data with

- **The other user in a booking:** the renter sees the owner's name and, after the owner accepts, the pick-up address. The owner sees the renter's name, dates and messages.
- **Everyone visiting the site:** listings, profiles, reviews and the approximate area of an item (not the exact address).

  - Render (website hosting) [location]
  - Sentry (error monitoring: error messages, page address, browser and a random user id; no names or emails) [location]
  - [Analytics tool, only if analytics is in use and you allow it] [location]
