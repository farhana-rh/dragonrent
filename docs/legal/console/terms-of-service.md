## 1. About DragonRent

1.1 DragonRent is an online marketplace where people in Finland rent items to each other. It is run by [Company name], business ID [Y-tunnus], [address] ("DragonRent", "we", "us").

1.2 People who offer items are **owners**; people who rent them are **renters**. One account can do both.

1.3 **DragonRent is not a party to the rental.** The rental agreement is made directly between the renter and the owner. We provide the platform, handle payments through our payment partner Stripe, and help when something goes wrong, but we don't own, inspect or deliver the items.

1.4 These terms apply together with the [Privacy policy](/p/privacy-policy), the [Cancellation and refund policy](/p/cancellation-policy), the [Damage policy](/p/damage-policy) and the [Cookie policy](/p/cookie-policy).

## 2. Your account

2.1 You must be at least 18 years old, or a company acting through an authorised person.

2.2 Give true and up-to-date information, and keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is done through your account.

2.3 Owners must connect a payout account with Stripe before their first item is published. Stripe checks the owner's identity as required by law; we can't publish items for owners who don't pass these checks.

2.4 We may ask for more information or check identities when we have reason to suspect misuse.

## 3. Listing an item (owners)

3.1 You may list only items that you own or are allowed to rent out, that are safe, legal and in working order, and that match your photos and description, including any wear or faults.

3.2 One listing is one item (or one set that is rented together), in the category that fits it.

3.3 You set the price for 1 day, and you may set lower prices for 3 and 7 days. Prices must be real and in euros.

3.4 Listings may not contain contact details, links to other sites, or rules that add costs, penalties, deposits or extra contracts outside DragonRent.

3.5 Items that may not be listed: weapons and ammunition, alcohol, tobacco and drugs, medicines, items that need a licence or registration to use (such as cars and boats), stolen or counterfeit goods, and anything else illegal to rent.

3.6 You are responsible for the taxes on your rental income and, where needed, for insuring your items.

3.7 We may edit, hide or remove listings that break these terms.

## 4. Booking (renters)

4.1 You choose the pick-up and return days and send a booking request. The full price is shown before you send it.

4.2 When you send the request, the amount is **reserved (held)** on your card. It is not charged.

4.3 The owner has up to 6 days to answer, and less when the pick-up day is sooner. If the owner says yes, the booking is confirmed and your card is charged. If the owner says no or doesn't answer in time, the hold is released and you pay nothing.

4.4 The person who books is the renter and is responsible for the item during the rental, also when someone else uses it.

## 5. Pick-up and return

5.1 The renter and the owner agree on the pick-up and return time and place in Messages. The exact address is shown to the renter after the owner accepts.

5.2 At pick-up, check the item together and take photos. The owner hands over the item as described, with everything listed under "What you get".

5.3 The renter uses the item with care, for its normal purpose and following the owner's instructions, doesn't lend it on, and returns it on time, clean and in the same condition.

5.4 **Late return:** tell the owner as soon as you know you'll be late. The owner can ask for the day price for each late day. [To be decided: how late days are paid.]

## 6. Prices, fees and payment

6.1 The price is the owner's price for the booked days. Longer rentals can use the owner's lower 3-day or 7-day price; the total shown at checkout is what the renter pays.

6.2 **Renters pay no service fee.** If this changes, any fee is shown before a request is sent.

6.3 Owners pay DragonRent a commission of 10% of the rental price (at least €1), taken before payout.

6.4 Payments are handled by Stripe. DragonRent never sees full card numbers.

6.5 The owner's payout is sent through Stripe two days after the return day. Stripe then needs a few working days to reach the owner's bank.

6.6 All payments for a rental must go through DragonRent. Asking for or making payments outside DragonRent is not allowed and can lead to account closure.

## 7. Cancellations and refunds

7.1 The full rules are in the [Cancellation and refund policy](/p/cancellation-policy).

7.2 Before the owner accepts, the renter pays nothing (section 4.3).

7.3 A renter can cancel for free within 24 hours of sending the booking request, by contacting us. When DragonRent cancels a booking, the renter gets a full refund to the card used.

7.4 After those 24 hours, a booking can't be cancelled by the renter and the rental price isn't refunded, in full or in part. This doesn't apply if the owner cancels or doesn't hand over the item: the renter then gets a full refund. Users can't cancel in the app; DragonRent applies these rules.

7.5 **Right of withdrawal:** by sending a booking request, the renter asks for the rental to start on the chosen day and accepts that the 14-day right of withdrawal for distance contracts doesn't apply to a rental for a fixed date.

8.1 The full rules are in the [Damage policy](/p/damage-policy).

8.2 The renter is responsible for loss of the item, theft, and damage caused on purpose or by carelessness, or by use against the owner's instructions. Normal wear is not damage.

8.3 Report damage, loss or theft to the owner in Messages straight away, and to us within [7] days of the return day.

8.4 DragonRent offers **no insurance and takes no deposit**. [To be decided: who pays for accidental damage that is nobody's fault.] Owners should check whether their home insurance covers items they rent out.

8.5 If an item isn't returned and the renter can't be reached, we may pass the renter's details to the owner and the police.

## 9. Reviews and messages

9.1 After a rental, the renter and the owner can review each other for 7 days. Reviews are published when both have reviewed, or when the 7 days end.

9.2 Reviews must be honest, about the rental, and free of insults or personal data. We may remove reviews and messages that break the law or these terms, but we don't change their content.
