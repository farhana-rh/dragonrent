## In short

- You can cancel for free **within 24 hours of sending your booking request**.
- **After 24 hours, the booking can't be cancelled** and the rental price isn't refunded, in full or in part.
- If the owner cancels, or doesn't hand over the item, you always get all your money back.

## How to cancel

You can't cancel in the app. Email us at [email] within 24 hours of sending your request, with the item's name and the booking dates. The time your email reaches us counts. [To be confirmed: times are Finnish time.]

[To be decided: if the pick-up day starts less than 24 hours after the request, whether free cancellation ends when the pick-up day starts.]

## Within 24 hours

- **If the owner hasn't answered yet:** we withdraw your request. The hold on your card is released and you pay nothing.
- **If the owner has already accepted:** we cancel the booking and refund the full amount to the card you used.

## After 24 hours

- We don't cancel the booking, and the rental price isn't refunded. The owner is paid as usual.
- This is different from a request that ends without a booking: if the owner says no, or doesn't answer in time (up to 6 days, less if the pick-up day is sooner), the hold on your card is released and you pay nothing.

## If the owner cancels

If the owner can't rent the item after accepting, contact us. We cancel the booking and you get a **full refund**, at any time.

## If the item isn't as described or isn't handed over

Contact us within [24 hours] of the pick-up time. [To be decided: full or partial refund.] The 24-hour cancellation rule above doesn't apply here.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Stripe usually returns the money within 5–10 working days. A request that ends without a booking isn't refunded because it was never charged: the hold on your card is simply released.
