How it works

Three steps, one weekend project

  1. 1

    Find it nearby

    Search by what you need. Prices are per day, and longer rentals get cheaper per day.

  2. 2

    Request your dates

    Your card is only charged when the lender says yes. You'll see the pick-up address then.

  3. 3

    Pick up, use, return

    Meet, check the item together, bring it back on the last day. Leave a review for each other.

For lenders

What if something breaks?

Lending to a stranger is a reasonable thing to worry about. This is the process that applies to every rental, start to finish.

List an item

Listing is free. DragonRent keeps 10% of each rental, minimum €1.

  1. Verified borrower ID and a card hold

    Borrowers verify their identity before their first rental. The rental amount is held on their card when they request.

  2. Timestamped handoff photos

    Both of you photograph the item at pick-up and return. The photos are stored with the booking and used if there is a claim.

  3. Payout within 48 hours of the return

    Your earnings go to your bank account through Stripe once the item is back and the rental is complete.

  4. Damage review within 5 working days

    Report a problem within 24 hours of the return. We compare the handoff photos, and approved claims are paid up to €2,500.
